COLLAPSE RITUALS

Published: 1989.08.19 | Author: @extcache

Infrastructure collapses the same way bodies do—first in small tremors, then in full biomechanical arrest.

Every grid is a nervous system. Every city is a skull waiting to be cracked open. Abandoned malls echo with the memory of commerce and ammonia.

Ruin is not aftermath, it is revelation. The act of falling apart is itself an architecture. Collapse is ritual, and ritual is information encoded in decay.

Albert Speer once proposed “ruin value” as a design principle. Build so your ruins are beautiful. We’ve perfected the inverse. Our ruins are broadcast towers.

Concrete breathes failure. Rust transmits messages more honest than fiber. Cities die like animals—lurching, leaking, twitching out corrupted signals until the final blackout.
